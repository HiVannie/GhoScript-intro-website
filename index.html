GhoScript lets you read your notes during video calls, so your notes can stay with you without getting in the way of your presentation.

What it does

A private prompt on your screen

GhoScript gives you a clean reading surface for notes, scripts, reminders, and talking points while you speak. It is designed for presenters, founders, teachers, interviewers, and creators who want a smoother delivery.

How it helps

Stay focused while you present

  • Write or paste your script into the app.
  • Switch to read mode for a cleaner teleprompter view.
  • Adjust speed, font size, and opacity to fit your setup.
  • Keep your notes on your own screen while you talk.

Supported platforms

Built for common video-call workflows

GhoScript is built for macOS and commonly used alongside video-call and presentation tools such as:

Google Meet
Zoom
Microsoft Teams
Slack Huddle

Compatibility may vary depending on how a platform handles screen capture and sharing.
