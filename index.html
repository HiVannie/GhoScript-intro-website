Install

First launch (important)

  • Download and open GhoScript.dmg, drag the app into Applications.
  • First launch: right-click GhoScript.app → Open → Open. GhoScript is distributed outside the App Store and is not notarized, so a normal double-click will be blocked by macOS.
  • If macOS still blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • Look for GhoScript in the top menu bar, paste your script, press Space to auto-scroll.

Features

Built for presenting

  • Ghost Mode — invisible to screen sharing, with a green GHOST badge so you know you are safe.
  • Auto-scroll — smooth 60fps, 5–200 px/sec, plus arrow keys, trackpad and mouse wheel.
  • Readable your way — font 14–72pt, opacity 30–100%, reading guide line, dark high-contrast theme.
  • Always on top, menu-bar only — floats above every app, no Dock icon.
